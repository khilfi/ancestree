Hassan bin Ismail, known to everyone as *Acan*, was born in Kota Bharu in 1938, the eldest son of Tok Ismail and Nenek Fatimah.

## The railway years

He joined the railway at twenty and stayed until he retired, moving the family from station to station: Kuala Krai, then Gemas, then home to Kelantan.

## Notable accomplishments

- Station master at Gemas for twenty years
- Built the surau at Kampung Laut with his brothers
- Never once late for a train

## Early life

He grew up by the Kelantan river, the eldest of four, and walked to school with his brothers every morning.

![The river at Kampung Laut, as he remembered it](scene:river)

## Middle life

Thirty years on the railway, from Kuala Krai to Gemas. He married Mariam in 1960, and they raised their children in the railway quarters, where every neighbour knew the trains by their sound.

![Gemas station](scene:station)

## Later life

After he retired he kept a small garden behind the house in Kampung Laut, and taught his grandchildren to fish.

## Character and mannerisms

Quiet, patient, and always first to laugh at his own jokes. He hummed while he worked, and checked his watch against the radio every evening.

## Sayings and quotes

- Sabar itu separuh daripada iman — to his children, often
- Jangan lupa asal usul — to Zul, the day he left for university
- A train waits for no one

## A message to the next generations

Look after one another. Whatever you become, remember the kampung that raised you.

## Life values kept for life

- Honesty, even when it costs
- Prayer at its time
- Family first

## Hobbies

- Fishing on Sundays
- Keeping songbirds
- Badminton with the neighbours

## Hidden talents

- Whistling like a bulbul
- Folding a sarong into a hammock in ten seconds

## Free notes

His railway watch is with Karim. His songbird cage hangs in Mariam's kitchen still.
