Rahman bin Ismail built fishing boats at Tumpat, by hand, from chengal wood.

## Later life

Widowed young, he married Halimah in 1977. In his last years he built model boats for every grandchild.

## Hidden talents

- Could carve a boat's whole hull by eye, with no drawing

## Free notes

The last boat he built, the *Siti Hana*, still fishes out of Tumpat.
