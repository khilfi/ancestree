Painem binti Sastro, Mbah Nem to her grandchildren, was born in Batu Pahat to a family that came from Ponorogo, in Java, a generation before.

## Early life

She grew up speaking Javanese at home and Malay at school, and helped her mother make kuih to sell at the morning market from the age of eight.

## Middle life

Her kuih lapis and onde-onde were known from Parit Sulong to the town. She sold them every morning for forty years.

## Sayings and quotes

- Alon-alon asal kelakon — slowly, as long as it's done; to anyone in a hurry
- Ojo lali karo asalmu — don't forget where you come from

## Life values kept for life

- Patience
- Hard work, done quietly

## Hobbies

- Javanese songs on the radio on Sunday nights
