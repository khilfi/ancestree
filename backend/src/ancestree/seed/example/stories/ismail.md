Ismail bin Ahmad, Tok Ismail to the whole kampung, farmed padi by the Kelantan river and led the prayers at the surau for forty years.

## Notable accomplishments

- Made the pilgrimage to Mecca by ship in 1952, three months each way
- Imam of the Kampung Laut surau, 1935–1975

## Early life

The only son of Ahmad bin Musa, a Quran teacher, he learned to recite before he could write, and to plough before he was twelve.

## Sayings and quotes

- Ilmu itu pelita hidup — to every child who came to learn

## A message to the next generations

Learn, and teach what you learn.

## Life values kept for life

- Generosity to guests
- Learning, at every age
