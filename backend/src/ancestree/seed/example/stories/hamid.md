Dato' Hamid bin Kassim began as a clerk at a tin mine near Ipoh, and ended with a building company of his own.

## Notable accomplishments

- Built the first terrace houses in his part of Ipoh
- Awarded the title Dato' in 1988
- Paid for the schooling of thirty children from the mining kampung

## Middle life

When the tin mines closed, he turned the mine's lorries into a building firm.

## Character and mannerisms

Stern at work, soft at home, and never without a pen in his shirt pocket.
