Mariam binti Daud was born in Muar in 1942 and came to Kelantan as a bride in 1960. Everyone calls her Mak Yam.

## Early life

She and her sister Salmah were raised by an aunt in Muar. She learned to sew on her aunt's machine before she could read.

## Middle life

In the railway quarters at Gemas she sewed baju kurung for half the town, and raised four children between the fittings.

## Later life

She lives in the house at Kampung Laut, where her grandchildren come every Hari Raya, and her garden grows more than she can eat.

![Mak Yam's garden](scene:garden)

## Character and mannerisms

She hums the same three songs, and never sits down until everyone has eaten.

## Sayings and quotes

- Air dicincang tidak akan putus — when her children quarrelled
- Measure twice, cut once — to anyone holding scissors

## Hobbies

- Sewing
- Her garden: chillies, lemongrass, and roses she won't let anyone pick

## What people say
