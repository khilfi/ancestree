Changing what a relative's computer may do, and handing the family to someone else.

- **Change role…** In **Settings → Family folder**, beside each computer of the family's, the keeper can now choose **Trusted**, **Contributor** or **Viewer** again, without the relative leaving and joining again. Their computer changes within a minute while it's on, and tells them so.
- **Hand the family to someone else.** The keeper can now offer the family to another of its computers, a relative's or one of their own: once they accept on theirs, they keep it, the family folder moves to their Google Drive, and everyone's computer follows by itself. The old keeper's computer carries on as a relative, or leaves.
- **Nothing comes in, or goes, unlooked at.** Changes waiting from that computer are reviewed first. A computer that becomes a Viewer keeps its own changes in a backup before it shows the family as the keeper keeps it.

When you sign in to Google from AncesTree, Google says it hasn't verified the app: your family's Google project is made for one family, so it isn't. Choose **Advanced**, then **Go to AncesTree (unsafe)**.

**If you installed 0.1.0, 0.1.1 or 0.2.0**, install 0.5.0 from this page by hand: those versions don't know the key updates are signed with now, so they won't update themselves. 0.2.1 and later update themselves.
