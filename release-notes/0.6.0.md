The map in detail, and places named to the district.

- **Every town and kampung in Indonesia** is on the map now, as Malaysia's are, and towns of 1,000 people or more everywhere else. A village written by its name alone finds its place.
- **Districts:** where someone lives says their district too: *Kampung Laut, Tumpat, Kelantan*. The map's list names each town with its district, so two kampungs of one name in a state are no longer counted as one.
- **In a view-only copy,** the map draws the districts of Malaysia and Indonesia, and the states and provinces of the countries around, and names the towns and kampungs around your family's places as you zoom in.
- **The map in detail:** zoom in and the map shows the land as OpenStreetMap draws it: towns, kampungs, roads, rivers and districts, each in its own name, with the English beneath where it differs. AncesTree fetches the world's map the first time you open it, 45 MB, once; **Settings → Map** fetches Malaysia with Singapore and Brunei (168 MB), or Indonesia (338 MB), for close up. Each is checked before it's used, and nothing is looked up online as you use the map.
- **Online detail, if you want it:** beyond the packs, such as a town abroad close up, turn on **Online detail where the packs end** in **Settings → Map**, and AncesTree asks OpenFreeMap for those parts of the map as you look at them. It's off unless you turn it on, and **Settings → Map** says what it tells OpenFreeMap: which parts of the world you look at, never anything about your family.
- **Who made the map:** its corner names OpenStreetMap's contributors and the others, and **Settings → About** says each one's licence.
- **No heavier for it:** AncesTree reads a country's places only once someone in the family has a place there.
- **Signing out of Google on one computer** no longer signs out your other computers with the same Google account, such as your own laptop: AncesTree now forgets the sign-in on that computer alone. Your Google account's own page still takes AncesTree's access away from every computer at once.

When you sign in to Google from AncesTree, Google says it hasn't verified the app: your family's Google project is made for one family, so it isn't. Choose **Advanced**, then **Go to AncesTree (unsafe)**.

**If you installed 0.1.0, 0.1.1 or 0.2.0**, install 0.6.0 from this page by hand: those versions don't know the key updates are signed with now, so they won't update themselves. 0.2.1 and later update themselves.
