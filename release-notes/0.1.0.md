The first release of AncesTree as a desktop app, for Windows, macOS (Apple Silicon) and Linux.

- One window, with the family on rings, as a family tree, on a timeline and on a map, and an icon by the clock.
- It can start when you sign in, and it makes a backup each day.
- Its own database comes with it: fetched and checked the first time it starts, with nothing else to install.
- Updates are signed, and installed when you restart.
