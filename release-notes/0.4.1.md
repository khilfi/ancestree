Safer for every family, now that anyone can download AncesTree for their own.

- **Files in the family folder stay where they belong.** AncesTree brings down only files named as it names them, and never writes outside its own folder, whatever a file in Google Drive is called. Before, a file named in a certain way in a relative's folder could have been written elsewhere on the keeper's computer.
- **A backup from someone else can't choose where its family goes.** **Add a family from a backup** makes the family's folder inside AncesTree's own, always. A backup made to fill the disk as it's read is refused before it can.
- **Nothing from the internet in the app's window.** A picture from the internet in a life story no longer loads, so it can't tell anyone the story was opened. Nothing the window shows can reach anything outside your computer.
- **On the Mac and Linux, the family's files are yours alone,** never readable by other accounts on the same computer.
- **A locked view-only copy says nothing of its family until it's opened**: not in its file name, nor in its title. Its password needs 10 characters now.
- **Spreadsheets open safely in Excel**, whatever a name starts with, in every region's Excel.
- **Very large photos** (over 80 megapixels) are refused before they're read, rather than slowing the computer.
- **A relative's sending that can't be read** no longer holds up the keeper's list of changes: **Take none** answers it.
- **How AncesTree is built** is safer too: what builds it can't change the code or the release, and each file's origin is recorded by GitHub before it's signed.

If you find a way around any of this, please say so privately: see **SECURITY.md** on this repository's page.

When you sign in to Google from AncesTree, Google says it hasn't verified the app: your family's Google project is made for one family, so it isn't. Choose **Advanced**, then **Go to AncesTree (unsafe)**.

**If you installed 0.1.0, 0.1.1 or 0.2.0**, install 0.4.1 from this page by hand: those versions don't know the key updates are signed with now, so they won't update themselves. 0.2.1 and later update themselves.
