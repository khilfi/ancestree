The guide inside AncesTree, written for everyone, and families who married in that never overlap.

- **The guide, without the internet:** click **?** at the top of the window, or open **Settings → Help**. It's the same guide as on GitHub, for the version you have, with its contents beside it, a search, its own links between parts with **Back**, pictures that open larger, and **Print** for a part, to print for a relative. **The newest guide, online** opens GitHub's.
- **Families who married in never overlap.** Their families open out as fans from the person who married in, their parents nearest. To see them another way, choose under **Married-in families** in the tree's **Rings** menu: **Discs**, **Settled fans** or **Tucked in**. Each computer keeps its own choice.
- **Lines to families who married in go around people.** The line from a married-in family to the person who married in finds its way between the others, never over anyone's photo, and crosses as few other lines as it can.
- **How does this work?** beside the harder places, such as the family folder's panels, **Changes waiting**, **Backups**, **Import** and **Hand the family to someone else…**, opens the part of the guide about them.
- **The guide, written for everyone:** it starts with **Start here**, which says which parts you need, and a **Quick start for relatives**. Each task says what it's for, who does it and what you need first, then the steps, one at a time, with the buttons' words as AncesTree shows them, and what to do if it doesn't work. Diagrams show how the family folder works, and pictures show each of its screens.
- **The invitation to send** shows the whole invitation, with **Copy** in reach: its long line could run past the window's edge, taking **Copy** with it.

When you sign in to Google from AncesTree, Google says it hasn't verified the app: your family's Google project is made for one family, so it isn't. Choose **Advanced**, then **Go to AncesTree (unsafe)**.

**If you installed 0.1.0, 0.1.1 or 0.2.0**, install 0.7.0 from this page by hand: those versions don't know the key updates are signed with now, so they won't update themselves. 0.2.1 and later update themselves.
