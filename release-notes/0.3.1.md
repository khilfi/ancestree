The family folder made sturdier, before relatives rely on it.

- **A new recovery code.** Lost yours, or someone else may have seen it? In **Settings → Family folder**, click **Lost your recovery code?**, then **Make a new recovery code**. Once it's in your Google Drive, the old one opens nothing.
- **The recovery code waits for you.** AncesTree shows it until you click **I've kept it safe**, even if AncesTree is closed and opened again in between.
- **The keeper on a new computer, safely.** **Be the keeper again** brings the family back, and waits until every photo and story has arrived before it sends anything. The old computer, if it's still on, stops keeping the family by itself, and says so.
- **Leave the family folder.** On a relative's computer, **Settings → Family folder → Leave the family folder…**: the family stays there, as their own. A relative who asked to join the wrong family, or was turned away by mistake, can leave and ask again. A computer whose AncesTree was copied from another computer now says so, and offers to leave, rather than not starting.
- **Changes that can't be read can still be turned down.** The keeper clicks **Take none**, and the relative hears that none of it was taken.
- **Plain words when Google or Google Drive says no**, and Drive asked again when it's busy. A relative's folder that Drive won't give just then no longer holds up the rest of the family.
- **On a relative's computer**, its own Trash, and where people were moved on its own tree, stay when the family arrives. The Malay birth-order titles are the keeper's: they're shown there, not changed.
- **One family folder to a Google account**: AncesTree won't start a second in the same Drive. The keeper's own changes, and a relative's brought in, are recorded apart.

When you sign in to Google from AncesTree, Google says it hasn't verified the app: AncesTree is made for one family, so it isn't. Choose **Advanced**, then **Go to AncesTree (unsafe)**.

**If you installed 0.1.0, 0.1.1 or 0.2.0**, install 0.3.1 from this page by hand: those versions don't know the key updates are signed with now, so they won't update themselves. 0.2.1 and 0.3.0 update themselves.
