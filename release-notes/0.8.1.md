Each place where it's meant, in a person's form; and clean updates on Windows.

- **Where they live, first:** a person's form now starts their places with **Where they live now**, the place the map shows, then **Where they were born**. **Living or deceased** sits beside the name, and the death part, **Where they died** and **Where they rest**, opens only for someone who has died. Each place is one line, such as *Kajang, Selangor · at its town*, until you click it, with a sign of its own: a house, a cradle, a stone. **Same as where they live**, **Same as where they were born** and **Same as their parents'** fill one place in from another.
- **Asked before saving:** if someone living has a place of death, or an address where they were born and none where they live, AncesTree asks first. **Move it to Where they live** puts the address and its pin where they live, keeps the town where they were born, and clears a living person's place of death. **Settings → What's missing** finds any saved this way already, with the same fix in one click.
- **The map's Where they died:** **Show** on the map now has **Where they died** too.
- **Clean updates on Windows:** each update on Windows had left the earlier version's files beside the new ones, so AncesTree's engine said it was 0.3.1. Now an update replaces it whole, and this one takes away what earlier ones left. **Settings → About** gives the engine's version beside the app's.
