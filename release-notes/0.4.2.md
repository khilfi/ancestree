Safer still, after the review that made 0.4.1.

- **An invitation is signed by your family's keeper.** When your keeper moves the family to another Google project, your AncesTree shows the project's name and asks before it signs in through it. An invitation your keeper didn't sign can't move your computer anywhere.
- **Photos keep no camera details.** Where a photo was taken, and with what, is taken out as it's added, and from the photos you have already, once. It never travels in a backup, the family folder or a copy.
- **Each release's file sums are signed** with the same key as updates, so anyone can check a download: see **Checking a download** in **SECURITY.md** on this repository's page.
- **The settings AncesTree's own tests use** no longer work in a release, so no other program can steer it with them.
- **A family's database password** is never on a command line, even for a moment, on its first start.

When you sign in to Google from AncesTree, Google says it hasn't verified the app: your family's Google project is made for one family, so it isn't. Choose **Advanced**, then **Go to AncesTree (unsafe)**.

**If you installed 0.1.0, 0.1.1 or 0.2.0**, install 0.4.2 from this page by hand: those versions don't know the key updates are signed with now, so they won't update themselves. 0.2.1 and later update themselves.
