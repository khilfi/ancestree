The family's own AncesTree, on each of its computers, kept in step through Google Drive.

This release replaces 0.2.0, which was withdrawn: its update key was lost, so it could never have received updates. 0.2.1 is the same app, with a new key.

- **The family folder.** In **Settings → Family folder**, sign in to Google and start your family's folder in your Google Drive, as its keeper. On a relative's computer, join the folder the keeper shared with them. The keeper checks a short code with each computer before letting it in, and chooses what it may do.
- **The family arrives by itself** on each relative's computer, photos and stories too, while AncesTree is open.
- **Relatives' changes come to the keeper.** On a relative's computer, changes wait for the keeper, who reviews them and brings in what they tick, with a backup first and one step for Undo. What isn't taken goes back to them, with the keeper's note. A trusted computer's changes come in by themselves, unless they clash or take something out.
- **Who changed this**, on each person: their last changes, and which computer they came from.
- **Merge…** makes someone entered twice into one.
- **Everything in Google Drive is encrypted** on each computer first: Google keeps the files, but can't read them. See the [privacy policy](https://github.com/khilfi/ancestree/blob/main/PRIVACY.md).

When you sign in to Google from AncesTree, Google says it hasn't verified the app: AncesTree is made for one family, so it isn't. Choose **Advanced**, then **Go to AncesTree**.

**If you installed 0.1.0, 0.1.1 or 0.2.0**, install 0.2.1 from this page by hand. Updates are now signed with a new key, which those versions don't know, so they won't update themselves.
