On a Mac, AncesTree reaches Google again.

- **The family folder on a Mac:** since 0.8.0, AncesTree on a Mac couldn't reach Google, and said "No internet just now" while the browser beside it worked. On a Mac without Homebrew, it couldn't fetch its database at its first start either. AncesTree now checks the certificates of Google and every other site as the computer itself does, so whatever the browser reaches, AncesTree reaches too. A Mac updates itself to 0.8.6, then catches up with the family.
- **It says what's wrong:** when a computer can't check Google's certificate, AncesTree says so, and suggests checking the computer's date and time, rather than "No internet just now".
