One pin for each spot, standing its people up on a click, and pins only where confirmed.

- **One spot, one pin:** people within about 2 metres of each other on the map, the same house to anyone looking, are one pin, the eldest's photo with how many are there, named *Fatimah and 2 more*. A click stands them in columns above it, up to 8 to a column, each a click from their panel; more than 40 open as a list.
- **Closer in:** the map now zooms in as far as about 15 centimetres to a dot, so someone a few metres away, such as a child next door, has a pin of their own.
- **A pin like Google Maps':** someone whose place is confirmed is a pin, its point exactly on the spot, their photo or initials in its head. Anyone else is a round photo, *about here*. **Looks right** now confirms a place, and so does **Confirm**, beside *About here* in a person's form. **Settings → What's missing** lists those not confirmed on the map, each with **Confirm** or **Place it**.
