A new icon: the family leaf.

- **The family leaf:** AncesTree's icon is now a green leaf on a pale green square, its veins branching like a family, with a person at the end of each. You'll see it by the clock, in the taskbar and the Start menu, at the left of AncesTree's top bar, and in a view-only copy's browser tab. The guide's pictures show it too.
